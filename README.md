# World Layoffs SQL Data Cleaning & EDA Project

This project focuses on data cleaning and EDA using a global layoffs dataset from the years 2020 until 2023 in Microsoft SQL Server.

## Key Steps & Workflow

* **Data Cleaning:** Removed duplicate records, standardized text and date formatting, handled missing/null values, and removed unnecessary columns or rows to ensure data integrity.
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Executed CTEs, window functions, and rolling aggregation queries to identify trends, yearly company rankings, and sector impacts.

## Key Insights

* **Company Scale:** Amazon leads overall layoffs (18,150 total), followed by Google (12,000), Meta (11,000), and Salesforce (10,090).
* **Industries:** Consumer (45,182) and Retail (43,613) industries experienced the largest overall workforce reductions.
* **Global Layoffs:** The United States dominated overall job cuts with 256,559 total layoffs, vastly outnumbering second-ranked India at 35,993.
* **Yearly Leaders:** Uber led layoffs in 2020 (7,525), ByteDance in 2021 (3,600), Meta in 2022 (11,000), and Google in 2023 (12,000).
* **Funding vs. Survival:** Heavily capitalized entities like Britishvolt ($2.4B) and Quibi ($1.8B) raised billions in funding yet still suffered 100% workforce layoffs, proving high funding levels did not prevent total operational failure.
